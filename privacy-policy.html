Privacy Policy

DueWise (com.hexlumegames.duewise). Effective 4 October 2026. Developer: Hexlume Games, Çanakkale, Türkiye. Contact: hexlume.games@gmail.com

1. Summary

DueWise has no accounts and no servers. What you enter stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is advertising-related data handled by Google AdMob, described below.

2. Data you enter

Tasks, lists, notes, history, team contacts (names and e-mail addresses) and local backups are stored only on your device. We cannot see or access them. Sharing is always started by you through Android's share menu or your e-mail app, and goes only to the app and recipient you choose.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, app interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (UMP) and you can change your choice later in the app. Ads are personalized only if you consent where consent is required. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your daily to-do and upcoming-task summaries.
  • Run at startup and wake lock: to restore and deliver notifications after a restart.
  • Internet and network state, advertising ID: to load ads.

These permissions are not used to collect other data.

5. What we do not collect

No accounts, analytics, location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access. Fonts and app files are bundled with the app.

6. Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal data. Ad-related data is received only by Google as described above.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android Settings, Apps, DueWise, Storage deletes everything. We hold no data on servers.

8. Security

Your data stays inside the app's private storage on your device. Ad traffic is encrypted. No method is completely secure.

9. Children

DueWise is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a minor has used the app, contact us.

10. Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, No. 6698), the GDPR and UK GDPR, and the CCPA/CPRA, you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data and may object to or withdraw consent for processing. Because we do not hold your data, you can exercise these rights by deleting it in the app or on your device; for advertising data, contact Google or us. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be announced in the app or on the store listing.

12. Contact

Hexlume Games, Çanakkale, Türkiye. hexlume.games@gmail.com

Uygulama Google AdMob reklamları gösterir. Google; reklam kimliği, IP adresi, cihaz ve işletim sistemi bilgisi, uygulama etkileşimleri ve tanılama verilerini reklam sunmak, ölçmek ve sahtekârlığı önlemek için toplayıp kullanabilir. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve gerekli olan diğer bölgelerde Google'ın onay formu (UMP) ile izninizi isteriz; tercihinizi daha sonra uygulamadan değiştirebilirsiniz. İzin gereken yerlerde reklamlar yalnızca izin verirseniz kişiselleştirilir. Reklam kimliğinizi Android Ayarlar, Gizlilik, Reklamlar bölümünden sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz. Bkz. Google Gizlilik Politikası ve Google'ın hizmetlerini kullanan uygulamalardan elde ettiği verileri nasıl kullandığı.
